# The Journey of a Gold Bar

An editorial, interactive five-minute guide for NYU's course **Beyond the Brand: Harnessing Emerging Tech in Ethical Sourcing**. It follows gold from mine to market to explain where ethical risks may arise, why refining and mixing can make origin difficult to establish, how major sourcing rules differ, and what traceability technologies can and cannot demonstrate. The rule summaries and qualitative risk labels are educational starting points, not legal advice or measured risk scores.

## Sections

1. Hero: the gold-origin challenge
2. Interactive supply chain journey
3. Key ethical and financial risks
4. Comparison of four rules and frameworks
5. Technology capabilities and limitations
6. My takeaway
7. Official organization homepages and source note
8. Footer

## How to view

Open `index.html` in a modern web browser. The page is self-contained and does not require a build step, external fonts, images, libraries, or a web server.
